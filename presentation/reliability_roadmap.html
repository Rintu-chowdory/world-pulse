07 / Reliability, trust, and roadmap

Live does not mean unquestioned.

World Pulse favors transparent degradation: stale-but-labeled data is better than silent failure, and every signal keeps its source identity.

Trust model / analyst promise

The system should make its limits visible, not hide them behind confidence.

+
Source identity stays attached

Every normalized event retains provider, timestamp, source URL, and source-specific metadata.

≈
Staleness is labeled

Provider outages surface as source.error messages while the last healthy event state remains available.

!
AI uncertainty is explicit

Pulse AI does not invent casualties, damage, or impact when the event record does not contain them.
